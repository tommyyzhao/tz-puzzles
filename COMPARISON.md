# Whole-corpus comparison

Visual-v2 update: current demos use shapes, original illustrations and machine graphics instead of text-only tasks. “Loses portraits/visual evidence” below refers to original-game portrait/social/scene richness, not absence of any graphics: Archive now has simple identity portraits and mechanical snapshots. That does not establish equivalence to commercial visual deduction. Original corpus, mechanisms and evidence grades remain unchanged; see `STUDY.md` / `RESULTS.md` for version-specific limits.

Every frozen ID appears exactly once; tests enforce coverage. **Mechanism assignments are analytical hypotheses**, not genre labels or verified equivalences. Several mechanisms can coexist in a game. Source anchors and edition boundaries: `CORPUS.md`; primary evidence: `EVIDENCE.md`. “Not demonstrated” means consciously excluded, not uninteresting.

Mechanisms: **I** discover fixed laws through discriminating observations; **P** apply known laws under coupled constraints; **E** edit laws; **H** reconstruct history/identity/causes; **Q** infer/select an informative evidence query; **C** construct a reusable process; **R** recursive containment; **O** operational representation (subtypes NOT merged automatically); **S** semantic/language interpretation. Knowledge-gated progression is a structure, not a mechanism of its own.

Evidence: **F** narrow FIRSTHAND interaction; **D** DOCUMENTED primary description (precision varies; ledger states limits); **I/U** INFERRED from previous secondary research, relevant primary mechanics UNAVAILABLE in this pass. D never means cognitive fidelity supported. Blank mechanism = insufficient evidence, not absence of reasoning. All cognitive comparisons remain **STILL UNTESTED**.

## Consolidation, not flattening

- **Candidate shared core:** Witness-style fixed-law induction and a narrow component of LOK/Taiji can be studied as hypothesis revision, but their operators/feedback/semantics remain different. Only Panel's own law is checked.
- **Candidate shared core:** SpaceChem/Opus Magnum create persistent executable constructions. Bench studies one boundary invariant, NOT the full common design space.
- **Co-located, not merged:** history reconstruction and inferred evidence addressing share Archive's case, but have separate predictions and controls. `directory` removes addressing; `finding` removes causal diagnosis.
- **Rejected as faithful reductions:** law editing to a toggle switch; semantic inference to arbitrary label matching; all “representation changes” to a camera trick; knowledge progression to a standalone mechanism; reconstructing an event to rearranging objects; recursive worlds to nested menus.
- **Context still needed:** social meaning, narrative stakes, visual associations, semantic uncertainty, world-scale knowledge, cooperative communication, and emotional impact. This pass cannot establish which of those can safely be removed.
