How to play

  • Move with arrow keys or WASD, by tapping a tile next to you, or by swiping. Tap a farther tile to walk there.
  • Push stones and boxes by walking into them.
  • Arches are doorways. Walk into one to go inside.
  • Undo anything with Z (Shift+Z redoes). Rewind (R) puts a clearing back as it began, even one you finished. Pools you woke stay awake.

Everything else, the hills will show you.

Rewind which clearing?

It goes back as it began. Pools you woke stay awake, and Undo brings it back.
